Ged held a push-up position at the summit of a mountain, his body taut, his breathing steady. 

Across his back lay a jagged slab of stone. From a distance it appeared to be ordinary rock, but up close it revealed itself as the severed tip of a mountain, hacked free and resting precariously atop him.

At the bottom of each rep, his chest brushed the ground. He lingered there for a heartbeat, muscles trembling, before driving upward again, wasting not a single motion. At seventeen, his strength and discipline surpassed even the most seasoned bodybuilders. Many called him a prodigy, his lifting feats stirring looks of admiration and envy from his peers. Yet to Ged, such praise meant very little.

He bore a rare affliction known as the Mind Shadow. Over time, it flooded his thoughts with shadows, the pressure mounting within his skull until it threatened to rupture. Left unchecked, the buildup would erupt in a single, catastrophic instant, ending his life without warning.

The only way to delay this fate was to strengthen his body faster than the Mind Shadow could swell. That relentless race left no room for rest. His life balanced on the edge of a blade, and even the smallest shift in his mood could trigger an abrupt end.

A sudden darkness swept across the mountaintop, blotting out the sun. Ged froze mid–push-up, eyes lifting skyward. An immense meteor, large as a moon, plummeted toward the horizon, its shadow swallowing the land.

“Finally. I’ve been waiting all day for this,” he murmured. Rolling his shoulders, he rose, letting the massive stone slide from his back. It thundered down the slope, unsettling rock and trailing plumes of dust in its wake.

“I wonder which Cosmic Bodybuilder is behind this month’s Celestial Meal.” His gaze narrowed as the meteor drew closer.

Far beyond the atmosphere, an immense figure loomed. His physique was so vast it could be seen from the ground. His body shone with such brilliance that it cut through the darkness of the eclipsed sun, the only light in the sky.

He moved in a blur, striking poses like a bodybuilder on stage. Each stance was masterful, carrying such profound force that ripples spread across the heavens, cracks spidering across the meteor’s surface.

Ged’s gaze traced every contour, hunger and determination burning in his eyes. “Long muscle bellies in the arms… flawless delt separation… there’s no mistaking it. That form belongs to Celestial Koh!”

Koh reached the climax of his routine. The meteor quaked, fissures racing outward. Straightening, he threw both biceps wide in a grand, heavens-shattering flex. The instant his muscles locked, the meteor burst apart, scattering into a thousand molten fragments that streaked toward precise targets across the globe.

One fragment hurtled nearby, its destination the nearby town of Elpida. 

Ged’s gaze studied the fragment, his jaw tightening. “Not good… all I see are low-grade celestial rock deposits. Not a single chunk of Star Obsidian.” He exhaled a weary sigh.

He turned toward Elpida.

From afar, the city resembled a colossal, shattered stone bowl. Jagged walls enclosed crumbling buildings, their dilapidated structures better suited to shelter cockroaches than house humans. Roads splintered into winding veins, all leading to the city’s heart: a vast, fog-shrouded pit known as the Abyssal Mouth.

Across the Planet Blue Eagle, pits like this were a common sight. Its many mouths gaped in silent hunger, waiting to swallow the celestial meal hurtling toward them.

_Bang!_

The meteors slammed into the Abyssal Mouths all at once, unleashing a deafening roar. Tremors ripped through the land, splitting the earth as magma surged from the fresh cracks.

Ged clamped his hands over his ears.

Dust choked the air, staining the sky a sickly orange. Far below, a colossal rumble rolled through the earth as molten stone ground in the jaws of something beyond comprehension.

The pressure inside his skull spiked. Shadows pooled in his vision, the Mind Shadow stirring like a beast jolted from slumber. Each heartbeat felt heavier, dragging him toward the brink.

His expression hardened. His core began to glow faintly blue as he triggered _Abs Storage_. Light rippled across his midsection, the surface of his abs turning glassy and still, like a perfectly calm lake. Reaching inside, he pulled free a corked bottle containing three jagged black pills.

He clenched the bottle until his knuckles whitened, his eyes bloodshot from the smoke.  
“Damn it all… why must this planet chew so loud?”

A vein throbbed at his temple. His composure shattered, the whites of his eyes turned an abyssal shade of black. Pain lanced through his skull as glowing red fissures tore across his scalp.

Blood streaked from his eyes as he dropped to his knees, fumbling the cork free. The bottle slipped once in his trembling hands before he barely forced a pill into his mouth. He swallowed dry; the jagged edges raked his throat raw.

Shadows receded slowly. Each breath rasped in his chest, but he held himself upright as the hellish ceremony dragged on.

A grotesque swallowing sound rippled across the land, stilling the chaos for a single heartbeat.

From deep within the planet, a thunderous voice boomed:  
“Celestial Koh, your meal is much appreciated. For your contribution, you will be rewarded accordingly. The ceremony is finished; all those who have been waiting patiently may now clean up the scraps.”

In the heavens, hundreds of Cosmic Bodybuilders appeared, orbiting the planet like constellations. In perfect unison, they flexed their cores and inhaled. The skies twisted into typhoon patterns as an incredible sucking force stripped the loose debris from the surface.

Ged ducked into a narrow crevice, wedged himself between two rocks, and braced against the storm.

Moments later, the winds died, the heat drained away by the celestial giants. With nothing else of value to extract, the Cosmic Bodybuilders vanished, and the planet began to settle.

When the last tremor faded, Ged emerged from the crevice. The cracks on his scalp sealed; the whites returned to his eyes.

After a time, he sat with the pill bottle resting in his palm. His gaze tired.  
“I can only make it halfway through a Celestial Meal before my Mind Shadow flares up… this is only a slight improvement from last time.” 

A wry smile tugged at his lips as he studied the two remaining pills.
“My mind is still too weak to operate on its own. Without Star Obsidian to make more pills, I doubt I can last much longer.”

His eyes lifted toward the Abyssal Mouth at the heart of Elpida, a flicker of resolve sparking in their depths.

He slid the vial back into his abs, brushed the dust from his clothes, and rose.  
“I hate fighting over scraps, but I have no choice. I’ll venture into the depths of the Mouth and take whatever I can.”

With that, he turned from the peak and began his descent down the mountain.

Blue lines flared along his calves as _Goat Walk_ activated. Overhead, a goat effigy took shape, and his feet clopped against the stone like hooves, gripping it like a goat on a sheer cliff, carrying him swiftly down the slope.

After the Celestial Offering, jagged shards had thrust through cooling magma, leaving a dangerous stretch of spikes and hissing steam between him and Elpida. With _Goat Walk_, he skimmed from shard to shard, leaping over boiling vents and crossing the broken terrain with practiced ease.

The city gate came into view. Ged slowed, his gaze narrowing at the scene ahead.

A bodybuilder in a suit was hurrying toward the entrance when the earth beside him ruptured. The ground split wide, and a car-sized worm burst forth in a shower of dirt and stone.

The worm’s mouth yawned open, a ring of serrated teeth glistening in the light. Inside, seated like pilots in a cockpit, were two bodybuilder thugs: one fat, one thin.
