The sky tore open. Darkness swept across Planet Blue Eagle, eclipsing the midday sun.

On the peak of a dumbbell-shaped mountain, Ged froze mid–push-up, eyes lifting skyward. An immense meteor, vast as a moon, plummeted toward the horizon, its shadow swallowing his place of training.

“Finally. I’ve been waiting all day for this.” He rolled his shoulders and rose, the massive rock on his back tumbling free. It thundered down the slope, shattering stone and trailing plumes of dust in its wake.

“The Celestial Meal has arrived.” He stretched out his body, watching the unfolding performance with anticipation.

Far above him, an immense figure loomed. His physique was so vast it could be seen from the ground. Reflecting the sunlight, he shone with such brilliance that it cut through the darkness.

He moved in a blur, striking poses like a bodybuilder on stage. Each flex of his muscles carried with it a profound power, sending rippling energy outwards, spidering the approaching meteor with visible cracks.

Ged traced every contour of the titan’s physique, eyes burning with recognition. “Long muscle bellies in the arms… flawless delt separation… there’s no mistaking it. That form belongs to Celestial Koh!”

Koh reached the climax of his routine. The meteor convulsed, fissures racing outward. Straightening, he threw both biceps wide in a heavens-shattering flex. The instant his muscles locked, the meteor burst apart, scattering into a thousand molten fragments that streaked toward precise targets across the globe.

One blazing shard tore through the sky toward the nearby city of Bowl, its target a vast fog-shrouded pit located at its center.

Ged clamped his hands over his ears, as he braced himself for what was to come.

_Bang!_

The meteor fragment struck the pit dead center, unleashing a deafening roar once it collided with its depths. Tremors ripped through the land, splitting the earth as magma surged from the fresh cracks.

Dust choked the air, staining the sky a sickly orange. 

Far below, a colossal rumble rolled through the earth as molten stone ground in the jaws of something beyond comprehension.

Ged's expression hardened. His core began to glow faintly blue as he triggered the muscle ability, _Abs Storage_. Light rippled across his midsection, the surface of his abs turning glassy and still, like a perfectly calm lake. Reaching inside, he pulled free a corked bottle containing three jagged black pills, just in case things got too ugly.

He clenched the bottle until his knuckles whitened, his eyes bloodshot from the smoke.  
“Damn it all… why must this planet chewing be so loud?”

Hidden inside his brain was a strange shapeless, shadow. Seemingly tied with his mood, it pulsed with life as the irritation mounted within him, causing immense pressure to build up against his skull. A disease he had to toil with since childhood, Ged had been fighting against its influence, but whenever immense stress took its toll, all it took was one crack for everything to come crumbling down.

A vein throbbed at his temple. His composure shattered, the whites of his eyes turned an abyssal shade of black. Pain lanced through his skull as the shadow caused blood-red fissures to tear across his scalp.

Blood streaked from his eyes as he dropped to his knees, the cork free. The bottle slipped once in his trembling hands before he barely forced a pill into his mouth. He swallowed dry; the jagged edges raked his throat raw.

The pills worked almost instantly and the shadow began to recede. Each breath rasped in his chest, but he held himself upright forcing himself to stay conscious.

Soon after the planet ceased its chewing. A grotesque swallowing sound rippled across the land.

From deep within the planet, a thunderous voice boomed:  
“Celestial Koh, your meal is acknowledged. For your contribution, a comparable reward shall be delivered." 
Koh nodded in response. Seeing that things were now settled the planet continued its announcement.
"I have finished my meal! All those who have been waiting patiently are now permitted to pick up the scraps.”

In the heavens, more unfathomable bodybuilders appeared, orbiting the planet like satellites. Their jaws spread wide, they inhaled. The skies twisted into typhoon patterns as an incredible sucking force stripped the loose debris from the surface.

Ged ducked into a narrow crevice, wedged himself between two rocks, and braced against the storm.

Moments later, the winds died, the heat drained away by the celestial giants. With nothing else of value to extract, the Cosmic Bodybuilders vanished, and the planet began to settle.

When the last tremor faded, Ged emerged from the crevice. His scalp healed, his countenance improved.

After a long silence, he sat with the pill bottle in his palm, gaze heavy.  
“I still can’t last an entire meal without a flare-up. And the Mind Shadow… it’s grown fiercer. I feel as if I have even less control of it compared to last time." 
His grip tightened.
"A month of relentless body and mind training, and what did I gain from it? Nothing.”
“At this pace… I don’t have much time left.”

Hopelessness pressed on him, but he clenched his jaw. His eyes lifted toward the fog-shrouded pit at the heart of Bowl, a flicker of resolve sparking.

He slid the pills back into his abs and brushed the dust from his clothes.  
“I hate fighting for resources at the Mouth. But money is tight and work is scarce. If what I scavenge buys me even a single extra pill… it will be worth it.”

With that, he turned from the peak and began his descent.

Blue lines flared along his calves as the muscle ability _Goat Walk_ activated. Overhead, a ghostly goat effigy took shape. His feet struck the stone like hooves, gripping the cliffside, carrying him swiftly down.

After the Celestial Offering, jagged shards had risen through cooling magma, leaving a hazardous stretch of spikes and steaming vents between him and Bowl. With _Goat Walk_, he bounded shard to shard, leaping over molten fissures and crossing the broken terrain with practiced ease.

Bowl loomed ahead. Its immense stone walls curved skyward, six equally spaced cracks serving as gates, where throngs of people pressed in from every direction.
