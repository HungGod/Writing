Ged's heart quickened. For his Mind Shadow hadn't stirred in several hours, he had almost forgotten it was there.

The man's annoyances made him realize just how fearsome he was. For the agitation he caused him was abnormal. 

_He's not human. A devil in disguise._

He forced himself to calm down, but he didn't take his eyes off the man. He watched his every action, trying to understand his motives.

The woman bodybuilder had just relinquished her seat to the man and moved to a different seat.

The man plopped down pridefully in the woman's seat. He held up his chin and returned his gaze back on Ged.

His expression changed.

"Hey, that's not right. How come you aren't mad at me anymore?"
