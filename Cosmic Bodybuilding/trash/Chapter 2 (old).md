Blue lines flared along his calves as he activated his muscle ability, Goat Walk, a technique that allowed him to grip even the steepest terrain like goat navigating a mountain trail. With the ability engaged, he began his descent.

Between Ged and Elpida stretched a vast, rocky field. 

Because of the cataclysmic events from the Celestial Offering, jagged shards of stone jutted from the ground, breaking through the cooling magma as plumes of steam billowed into the air. 

Yet because of Goat Walk, Ged nimbly bounced from one stone to the next, making the deadly journey seem like a walk in the park.

As Ged approached the city entrance, his pace slowed until eventually he came to a halt. 

At the gate, a bodybuilder in a suit and tie was hurrying toward the entrance.

But Ged’s focus wasn’t on the man—it was on the ground stirring nearby.

The earth split open, revealing a car-sized worm. Its jaw unhinged, exposing a gaping maw lined with rows of serrated teeth.

The suited bodybuilder froze in his tracks.

Inside the worm were two bodybuilder thugs, one fat and one slim.

“One kilogram, and you can keep your legs,” the fat thug slurred through broken teeth, he flexed his bicep, waving his gun menacingly.

The suited man's expression turned fearful.

This wasn’t his first time dealing with something like this.

Eager to end the confrontation quickly, he reached into his abs, pulled out a kilogram of common stone, and tossed it to the fat thug.

Grumbling in frustration over the exchange, the suited man turned to leave.

But before he could get far, he was stopped in his tracks a second time.

“I didn’t say you could leave," the thin thug snickered, stretching out a hand expectantly.

"Aren’t you forgetting to pay me as well?”

The suited man gritted his teeth.

But what could he do?

He was a working man in the public sector; his muscles were built for supply-chain management, not combat.

The grueling demands of his job left no time to train. And even if he had the time, it wouldn’t matter.

The regulations of the Elpida Governing Body strictly prohibited the development of combat-related muscle groups.

As humiliating as it was, he could only pay the fee. Once again, he reached into his abs and drew out another kilogram of common stone, placing it in the thin thug’s waiting hand.

“Can I please leave?” the suited man muttered, clenching his fists as he stared angrily at the two thugs.

The thugs exchanged mocking laughter, reveling in his misery.

“Yeah, you’re all square. Have fun at work, loser,” the fat thug laughed.

The worm's mouth closed as it burrowed just beneath the surface, leaving the suited man to simmer in his anger alone.

Inside the worm, the thugs reflected on the day's success.

“This city is amazing,” the thin thug snickered, counting the kilograms of stone in his hands. “I can’t believe how much mass we’ve made in just one outing.”

“Brother, trust me,” the fat thug said, his eyes lighting up like golden saucers. “This is merely the beginning."

"Elpida is just the first step in our rise to infamy. Soon, the entirety of Planet Blue Eagle will know the name of the devious Thug Brothers,” he declared proudly.

“Now come on, hurry up. Find us our next sucker,” the fat thug commanded.

The thin thug frantically scanned through a fleshy periscope device dangling from the roof of the worm's mouth. Through it, he could see a short distance beneath the earth. Adjusting the view, he scouted the surface.

“Oooh, this guy looks good,” he muttered as the periscope locked onto Ged. “With a physique like that. He’s gotta be loaded with kilograms.”

“Give me that,” the fat thug demanded, grabbing the periscope from his brother's hands.

“No gang tattoos. No government markings either..." His expression turned exuberant.

"Holy shit, what a jackpot!” His grin widened. “Let’s charge him five… no, ten kilograms for his life.”

With their target in mind, they directed the worm to surface.

The worm erupted from the ground in front of Ged, its gaping maw opening wide to reveal the two thugs, guns in hand, grinning viciously.

The fat thug began to speak.

“Giv—”

Before he could finish the word, a jagged rock shot toward his face, severing his head from his body and sending him slumping backward—dead.

Ged had anticipated the Thug Brothers’ arrival.

He had strategically positioned himself within kicking range of a loose, jagged stone, patiently waiting for the worm to rear its head. The instant they appeared, he delivered a perfect kick, launching the projectile with lethal precision.

Ged’s swift attack caught the brothers entirely off guard, instantly dispatching one of them. With the advantage now firmly his, he turned his attention to the surviving thug.

The remaining thug stared in horror, his eyes darting between his brother’s headless corpse and Ged, as terror slowly consumed him.

As Ged rapidly closed the distance, a single thought dominated the thin thug’s mind: escape.

Heat began to gather at the thug’s fingertips, blue energy bursting forth and streaming into the roof of the worm’s mouth. The energy spread swiftly, tracing pathways directly toward the creature's brain. Using this energy, he crafted a desperate command for the worm to burrow underground and flee.

But Ged wasn’t about to let him escape so easily.

Summoning blue energy of his own, Ged pressed his palm firmly against the worm, sending out a competing command.

A brief but intense struggle ensued, yet Ged’s prowess proved vastly superior. Within seconds, he overwhelmed the thug’s control.

The worm’s enormous form went limp as Ged effortlessly severed critical neural pathways linking its brain to its body. With a thunderous crash, the towering beast collapsed, its mouth frozen open, trapping the thug inside.

What he had just witnessed defied belief. To counter the thugs commands was no easy task. The thin thug and his now-dead brother had dedicated years to understanding and manipulating the worm’s neural pathways. Even considering the gap in their strength, such a thing was impossible without extraordinary capabilities.

Only one profession was capable of such a feat.

"Neuroalchemist…" The thug's eyes widened in horror.

With no other option, he raised his gun and fired.

“Die!”

Ged flexed his chest muscles, causing the bullets to ricochet harmlessly off him like pebbles striking a mountain.

The thin thug’s mouth dropped open in despair.

Ged approached, a deep frown etched on his face.

“Uh... wow... sir, your physique is so magnificent,” the thug stammered. “Had I seen it up close, I never would have dared to act so boldly. As for killing my brother, I’ve already forgotten about it.”

He knew it was useless to argue. Even with his brother’s death like a knot in his stomach, he understood resistance was futile. Neuroalchemists, the great inquisitors of the bodybuilding world, were not men to cross lightly.

Ged reached into his abs and pulled out a green frog-like device, leveling it at the thug.

The thug panicked, staring at the device in horror.

“No, please, Mr. Neuroalchemist, have mercy!” he pleaded. “There’s no need to showcase your mind-altering abilities. Whatever you want from me, whether its information or stones I’ll give it without resistance.”

Ged only sneered in response.

He channeled energy into the device. The tongue shot out, striking the thug between the eyes. Instantly, the man went limp.

Ged pressed his thumbs over the man’s scalp, reading the contours of his cranium, he could read some of the residual thoughts of the thug.

Ged took in each piece of information with a neutral expression, as if he had done this hundreds of time, but when he was glossing over the sections regarding why the thug brothers had come to extort in Elpida his brow furrowed.

As he delved deeper, he heard ocean waves lapping at the shore, in front of him appeared an enormous ocean, much like an illusion. Staring out beyond the horizon, the calming scenery caused him to lose his grip on the man, and the connection to his frog device became severed. 

The thug jerked back to awareness, gulping air. He blinked up at his captor—and found Ged utterly still, gaze distant, as if watching that horizon.

“Sir…?” the thug whispered.

No response.

“Great one?” Louder now.

Nothing. He rose, stepped close, even waved a hand across Ged’s eyes. No reaction.

Joy flooded him. “God is real! I’m alive!” He spun, half-cackling, and clambered into the worm, meaning to flee while Ged drifted.

At the threshold he glanced back. A new, mean thought bloomed.

“Maybe I’m being hasty,” he murmured. “I should help unburden this great one—by a kilogram or two.”

Greed curled his mouth as he looked at Ged like a plated meal.
