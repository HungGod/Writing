The sun peaked over the horizon, gleaming over the mountain fortress of Elpida, yet despite its blaze, it did little to warm the below freezing temperatures felt outside.

Underneath the ground, in a bunker home a black haired boy had already beaten the sun and was on the floor of his room, performing an exercise routine preparing for the day. Despite his weak body unable to perform a single push-up, he was persistent, pushing through even if what he was capable of performing could be constituted as a rep.

A knock came to his door, causing the boy to stop, his eyes narrowing at his bedroom door as it creaked open.
