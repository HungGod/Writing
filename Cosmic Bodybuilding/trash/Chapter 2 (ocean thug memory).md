Lost in the ocean memory fragment, Ged felt as though he were dreaming.

The world around him shimmered with golden sunlight, a warm breeze kissed his cheek. In this strange place he found this golden light cast no shadows, and when it shined upon his head it was like a heavenly tonic. His head no longer throbbed, his thoughts now were beautifully sharp and crisp.

In this strange memory fragment of the ocean, he felt the shadows in his mind unprecedentedly shrink in size. They continued to retract in form until it molded itself into a black seed. 

An intense soul-shaking relief flooded over him. A feeling he had prayed years to experience, to spend even a moment where he could be without the worry of death. The mind shadow was like a sword that hung above his head, yet now he could feel that sword retreating, the promise of death fading away.

Tears streaked down his face as he laughed jovially. “Master… you said there was no cure. That I should accept my suffering. But look! Look at this! It is here, I can feel it. Within this place holds the key to eradicate this sickness.”

He drank from the sight like a man dying of thirst. So rapt he didn’t notice the blood dripping from his stomach. The shadow seed within him shrank smaller, smaller still…

Ged's brow furrowed as realization struck him like thunder. The horizon was fading away from view. The fragment of the ocean was incomplete. A small piece of a much larger puzzle. The shadow seed could not be fully erased.

The vision trembled. The sunlight grew dim. The ocean blurred away.

“No!” Ged’s voice cracked. “Don't go!” He reached for the golden waves, but they were but smoke in his grasp. The smell of stone and blood emerged through the salty air as his eyes adjusted to Elpida’s bleak skyline, agony lanced through his abdomen. His hand pressed instinctively against the wound.

When he looked down, a river of blood was gushing out.

The dream shattered. He lay on cold stone, not sand. A silhouette bent over him, carving knife in hand, its movements steady and deliberate. With each cut, the figure pulled bloodied stones from a hidden cavity in his abs.

Ged’s gaze hardened as he tried to force away the daze and confusion. The ocean was now gone from view. Above him the thin thug was cutting through him, a handful of bloody stones lay in his palm, his grin had long since faded, and his greedy gaze was now darting back and forth filled with worry that he might have pushed things too far.

"Damn it!" The thug couldn't help but curse in frustration, he couldn't help but feel wronged by the whole situation, yet he wasn't willing to leave so soon. By his side several knives lay discarded, some of them had their tips bent at angles, some their blades had become too dull to cut through. Along Ged's neck and the space of his heart cavity, knife marks could be seen, numerous attempts by the thug to end his life, yet none seemed able to break the skin. Having given up on ending his life, and seeing that Ged was still unresponsive, with the last knife he brought with him he was able to find success barely cutting into his abs, allowing him entry into the storage space where he kept most of his goods. 

Yet this man was too poor, only five stones, two black pills, and a Soul Taser was present inside. Leaving the thug completely dumbfounded, not onlbastardy that but every stone was placed deep in the abdominal muscle, they were encased in tough to trim muscle fibers as if they were to be protected at all costs. Not only that, but as he collected his meager gains, he found Ged to be slowly emerging from his vision, his arms twisting, and even strange words were escaping from his mouth. Sweat dripped down his forehead, as he levered the knife against the tough flesh, digging out the final stone. 

At this point Ged's gaze was beginning to take inventory of his surroundings.

"You have some guts..." Ged spoke horsely as the silhouette became clearer in his vision, as he slowly processed what was happening around him, his anger mounted.

The thug wrenched free the last stone and rose up to his feet looking to flee—

But a hand clamped his ankle like a beartrap.

Ged’s eyes snapped open. Memories of the ocean surged back, colliding with the bleeding present. His brow furrowed, hatred igniting in his gaze.

“Sir—wait—!” the thug yelped, his voice shrill, words tumbling over each other. He tried to pull away, but Ged’s grip was iron.

Blue energy flooded from Ged's fingertips into the man's veins. The thug filled up like a balloon, he barely could let out a scream before his eyes rolled back, his body crumpled to the floor dead, his internals ripped to shreds.
