The sun shown brightly on the crescent-shaped mountain village of Elpida. 

Having gone through an especially cold winter, the warm sunlight from the summer sun was particularly refreshing, lifting the spirit of the townspeople and creating a contagious jovial atmosphere.

This was especially so for the Elpida High School's graduating class. Having attended classes in the same place since they were in diapers, many were itching for change, excited for their future and looking forward to exit the constricting and suffocating school and focus instead on their careers.

In a shoehorn theater, packed with the aforementioned students as spectators was a spectacular event. 

In the crowd was a sturdy boy with green hair, and sharp facial features, his eyes locked onto the stage with great interest. His name was Ged, and this performance meant a great deal to him and his fellow classmates, as it could play a big role for what they might end up doing in the future.

The first to step forward on the stage was a behemoth of a man wearing blue overalls and a straw cap. 

He turned to a nearby podium where a teacher holding a strange living microphone was announcing to the crowd.

"Here first comes Ol' Proctor. He is our local food producer, most of the meals we eat in Elpida can be attributed in some ways to him. He has the profession of Farmer, and will attempt to share a bit of what his life is like out on the fields." The teacher spoke enthusiastically.

Ol' Proctor flashed a grin, thanking the teacher for the introduction. Then without much speaking he began his performance.

His abdomen flashed with a strange blue light, his abs becoming still like the surface of a lake. A flash of his hand reached inside, from which he pulled out a metal dumbbell, presenting it to the crowd.

Holding the dumbbell steady in his right arm, Ol' Proctor's bicep muscles twitched in bizarre ways, different muscle groups knitting strands of blue energy like they were creating some strange fabric inside his arms, then he began to curl the dumbbell.

Once the first rep finished the skin over his bicep began to shiver, and a strange sight occurred, bursting out from his skin the stem of a newborn plant could be seen. The dumbbell in his hand shimmered brightly, as it seemed to increase in weight with every rep performed. 

Some of the crowd seemed to be moved by his actions, sounds of surprise and awe abound.

Ol' Proctor's face grew serious and he spat out the piece of grass in his mouth. Gritting his teeth he began to workout more vigorously, each rep causing the plant growing from his bicep to grow taller.

After ten reps the plant began to outline the husk of a corn crop, it seemed after a few more reps it would become harvestable.

The wooden floor beneath the Ol' Proctor began to give audible signs of strain, as the dumbbell seemed to have unfathomably heavier, but Ol' Proctor seemed largely unmoved by the weight change, focusing solely on his craft he finished off two more reps with finesse and ease.

The crop bloomed in full, the husk of corn atop its stem began to crack open showing the golden cob underneath. 

Ol' Proctor put away his dumbbell back into his abs, grabbed the cob using his free hand, taking a triumphant bite, grinning triumphantly while he chewed his freshly grown sweet corn, he gave the crowd a wave. 

The crowd of students erupted with claps of applause, clearly moved by the act.

"Give it up for Ol' Proctor!" The teacher emphatically gestured to the munching farmer. 

"Now Ol' Proctor's farm has three workers for which he is hiring for. Who here is interested in becoming a farmer? First three hands to be raised " In the teachers hands were glowing green stones with the Ol' Proctor's contact information, as the hands and the crowd were raised, the teacher whisked their hand and sent over the proper stone, soon all stones were passed out and Ol' Proctor walked off stage.

Ged looked over on his right one of his neighbors was one of the lucky ones to receive the farming invite, causing him to snort inwardly. Clearly he had no interest in farming.

Some of the students were a bit bummed out that they were unable to secure a stone. The teacher went on to placate their worries.

"Don't worry their will be plenty more opportunities for job stones going forward."

The show went on.

Two men appeared on stage dressed in firefighting gear, one lit themselves on fire through muscle flexation, the second performed a strange posing ritual causing a water hose to exit his chest and put out the fire.
