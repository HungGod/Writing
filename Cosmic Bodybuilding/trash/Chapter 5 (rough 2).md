His hands and feet stung with pins and needles. It was as if there were thousands of insects inside of him gnawing at his flesh.

"Your life and death is still uncertain. The Mountain can only be climbed through steps not leaps, it is best to pace yourself, and not fall into the despair of chasing the end. Focus first on reaching the fourth level of Muscle Condensation, then move on from there." 

Ged swallowed. 

"Steps not leaps?" Ged's face fell in disbelief, anger replacing his despair.

He had heard his father say lines similar to this hundreds of times. For any other situation he may have been able to tolerate such advice, but now he could not.

"Father. Be serious. If I just take it one step at a time, how can I possibly reach Mountain Establishment? I need to change up my workout routines, I have to acquire vast amounts of precious resources, I need to fast track permits, how can this all possibly be achieved in simple steps?" 

Ged's voice grew serious.

"I will not allow my life and death to be an uncertainty. I hope you can understand this."

Gore stroked his beard.

"Then I have nothing more to say." His tone final.

Ged gritted his teeth, his hands balled so tight blood stopped circulating. 

He stared at Gore, intensity burned like fire, yet as soon as they landed Gore's ethereal blue eyes and wooden expression they quenched.

_Damn it!_

He stood up from Mind Magnification Stone and in large bounding strides moved to the exit.

“You think so little of me. I wouldn’t dare be so cruel.” He chuckled. “My dear brother Ged is mischievous and reclusive. I only worry about him. "Just don't push things too far."

“It seems I can only rely on myself.”

_I need every resource I can find. No matter how meager, I’ll seize every last pebble without fail!_

For each tier special permits were required to stay within their limits, the elite tier was especially fierce, and the physique restrictions were minimal to almost none. Each tier connected to each other through snake train transportation with the Abyssal Mouth the final ring at the bottom.

Its insides hollowed out, rib cage bone passenger cars, could be seen through gruesome windows into flesh.

Fangless snake mouths stitched onto the side of its skin gaped wide acting as doors, allowing Ged to step inside.

The passengers inside were all vicious looking bodybuilders. Cigarette smoke filled his nostrils, eyes glaring at him from various sources.

Ged did not challenge any of the gazes, for he could tell at a glance that these bodybuilders were all born from the underground. They were the seedy types, and would kill someone over a pin dropping.

He found an open flesh chair and sat down. Closing his eyes, he didn't want to bother with anyone surrounding him.
