The sun beat down without obstruction, as sweat poured down the face of several bodybuilders were performing a series of bicep curls.

Among them was a young bushy green haired boy named Ged worked, focusing in on his bicep curls.

Yet the weights they moved around didn't seem to be of the purpose of increasing their physical prowess. Rather between the muscular physiques of those present were the buddings of agriculture, as the beginning forms of broccoli plants sprouted from their biceps.

These bodybuilders were vegetable farmers, and they were currently in the process of cultivating broccoli, and once they were finished the plants would be directly harvested from their physiques.

Supervising them was Ross, a man, whose hat did little to keep the sun from beating down on his lobster red skin.

He walked up and down the line, fixing the form of the many bodybuilders supplying produce for 

"Keep those elbows out, those Broccoli Bicep Curls look terrible..."

He swaggered to one of the farm hands, moving his elbows in place with a nudge of his hand.

"Too much necessary movement at the top will affect the appearance of your broccoli. At Ross's farm we do not sell ugly broccoli, fix it!..."

Each comment and following form adjustment was followed by a flawless correction and a quick, "yes, sir," causing Ross to hold a satisfied expression. 

Ged wasn't even midway though the routine and his arms were beginning to falter. His arms shaking as he struggled to lift his arms to complete a rep.

Compared to the others, Ged wasn't as physically imposing, and despite providing impeccable form, his broccoli looked pitiful.

"Ged!" Ross barked, "you are two reps behind the others, don't tell me its just because you are weak? At the rate you are going I won't even see a single mature broccoli made by you."

"Is this truly all you got?" Ross asked.

"No, sir, its not." Ged was quick to respond, gritting his teeth he tried to hide his weakness, hastily he performed two grueling reps.

"Good." Ross took a step back, "last week I may have been tolerant of your weakness, because I had hopes you would improve." He continued slowly. "But I am out of patience with you, if you fail to live up to expectations today, don't expect to keep having a job here moving forward."

Ged stared at the ground solemnly as Ross walked further away.

Swirling in his young mind were the many jobs he had fired from, the many opportunities squandered, due to him not being able to meet the moment.

In his heart he had, had enough. 

*This time is different! Today I refuse to fail!* 

His heart steeled as Ged tried his best to ignore the pain his biceps. Through effort alone he forced himself to catch up to his peers, the single stem of broccoli growing on his arms showing signs of maturity with each rep.

Fifteen more excruciating reps passed and the others surrounding him were just finishing off the final few reps to mature their broccoli.

*Thwack!*

Ross hovered near a veteran bodybuilders, knife in hand, he cut off the first broccoli. Admiring it in his hand.

"Excellent quality." He muttered happily.

*Thwack! Thwack! Thwack!*

Ross began to harvest the mature broccoli from the many bodybuilders, tossing each chopped produce in a nearby ice chest. 

First the high tier bodybuilders were harvested, their bodies were the size of small houses, their arms carrying thirty crops of broccoli each.

Then the mid tier bodybuilders were harvested, who sported just ten crops per arm, their frames spanning a mid-sized car.

Then the low tier bodybuilders were harvested, each were slightly larger than an average human, sporting three crops of broccoli each.

Ross walked around admiring each broccoli he harvested with pride, for not a single stem fell bellow "good quality" causing him to hum with joy.

That was until he came across the ametuer bodybuilders. Each bodybuilder here only had a single crop of broccoli to harvest. 

*Thwack.*

"Mid." He sneered, tossing the broccoli into the ice chest.

*Thwack.*

"Passable... I guess..." Ross tossed a sickly looking broccoli away.

...

Ross had finally reached Ged. On his arms were two meak looking crops of broccoli, not close to be able to be harvested.

Ged's eyes were bulged from his sockets, veins strewn across his face, as he struggled. Looking up at Ross, he could tell from a glance from his expression that what he produced was not enough.

"How many more reps do I need?"

Ross's eyes narrowed, then his expression softened.

"Ged, you're a good and honest kid, but I'm just afraid you are not cut out for this kind of work."

Ged steeled himself further and asked again.

"How many more reps?"

Ross stayed silent a moment, before finally whispering. "Two more."

Ged gave up on one of his arms, letting the broccoli plant whither and die, as he dropped the dumbbell to the ground, bracing his arm with his free arm. Giving up on producing broccoli from one of his biceps and focus all his efforts into his right arm. With great difficulty he forced out a curl.

"One." Ross counted out for him. "Go on, last rep."

The remaining on his arm grew more vibrant. Its top fuller, green leaves sprouting from its stem.

Ged went to curl again.

"Two--"  *Rip!*

Just as Ross was announcing the second rep, a ghastly tearing sound echoed out. 

Ged dropped the second dumbbell, involuntarily he screamed out in pain, as the pain of his torn bicep caused tears to stream down his cheek. 

*Thwack*

Ross moved quick harvesting the broccoli plant from Ged's arm, examining it closely, studying the fullness of the broccoli head.

"The size is... decent..." he flipped it around and examined the stem, where dark spots had formed. Indicating rot. "Unfortunately my hand wasn't quick enough and the harvesting failed..." 

"this broccoli cannot be sold."

Ross loosened the grip on Ged's broccoli letting it fall to the ground.

Ged watched the broccoli fall, as if in slow motion.

All the work he put in. The injury he had endured. What did it amount to?

The broccoli hit the ground with a whimper, its small size unable to even kick up dust or make any noise.

Ged had failed. His efforts--futile.

"Ged, turn in your company dumbbells," Ross continued to speak, his voice stern. "You won't be able workout anymore today with that kind of injury. Nor will you be working out at my farm going forward. If I am to stay competitive in this industry, I can't afford to pay someone who can't produce."

Ged's heart sunk and his body involuntarilly collapsed to the ground.

Ross turned to the other bodybuilders. 

"Let's take a break, in thirty minutes we'll reconvene and do some Pumpkin Split Squats."

Ross moved to attend to putting away the ice chests filled with harvested broccoli, leaving Ged by himself.

Ged sat there listless. His injured arm hung limp, searing with pain, yet Ross paid little attention. To him Ged's value was only use how many vegetables he could produce. 

The other farmers taking a break stood aimlessly by, awkwardly keeping their distance. 

Ged was well aware of their coldness.

After a few moments he pushed himself off the ground. Looking to leave this place behind, and figure out a plan for what he was to do next.

As he walked one of the high tier bodybuilders activated his "abs storage" reaching inside he tossed over a tube of Numbing Paste to Ged.

Ged caught it in his free hand and looked up at the farmer who threw it.

"You're frame is too thin, it was only a matter of time before your bicep tore. Its not that you are not cut out for farm work..." the man spoke honestly, pausing before finally saying. "You should give up on the path of bodybuilding."

Ged remained mute, unable to express gratitude for pain relief medicine. 

He turned his back on Ross's farm, applying the Numbing Paste to his torn bicep, but the quality was low, so the searing pain was only diluted by half.

Ged's calves glowed with blue light, as he activated the ametuer muscle ability, "Quickness." 

With the ability activated Ged moved swiftly across the rocky terrain on his way back to home.

In the middle of a rocky un-descript field a rusted vault door jutted out. Its entrance dipping down into a underground home bunker.

Ged approached the bunker his steps slowing. His fingers wrapped around the locking mechanism of the door, twisting with one hand, worry stricken his face.

Ged lifted the door with exceptional effort revealing a staircase leading downwards, bursting outwards an earthy wind escaped.

"Father, I'm home..." he spoke un-enthusiastically.

With small steps he made his way inside. 

A voice called out below. "Ged? So soon? What happened? Don't tell me you've been fired."

At the bottom of the stairs an older man hunched, his frame barely being held up by a walking cane.

Ged saw the worry in his fathers face and instantly his demeanor changed now wearing a less destitute expression.

"No, don't worry father, Ross just sent me home early to tend to a workout injury. I don't plan on staying long." Ged spoke.

Ged's father's eyes narrowed.

"Is that so..."
