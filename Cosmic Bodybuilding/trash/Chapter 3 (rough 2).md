Ged hurried through cracked sidewalks, his figure blended in crowds.

Although this was technically considered apart of Bowl, due to the constant meteor barrages the surface was largely underdeveloped filled with slum dwellings, for the ones that took refuge here were those that were unlucky enough to be left behind.

Untrained civilians. Their physiques gaunt and lifeless. Some were begging on the, others selling odds and ends. With the passing of the Celestial Offering many had their homes destroyed, most were currently in the process of rebuilding.

Ged paid little attention, he moved along, until he neared the underground entrance to the core of the city.

There two lines snaked to two different kiosks, where burly bodybuilder officers worked to arbitrage the people inside.

One was the express lane, the other the civilian entrance.

At the front of express lane, a large bodybuilder guard conducted physique inspections on those who entered, carrying large metal baton, he waved them over their various muscles, checking their density and total mass and collecting a stone's fee for entrance.

The line moved swiftly with little issue. 

Some young bodybuilder's quad muscle density didn't have the proper permit and measured above the civilian limits. But after apologizing profusely, he was quick to be acquitted of the incident.

This stood in great contrast to the civilian entrance. Howls of pain echoed out, as untrained civilians and lowly bodybuilders were tied up like pigs and beaten with batons by bodybuilders more than four times their size. 

There was a clear pattern. 

If you had enough muscle you generally passed with no problem, otherwise... good luck.

In front of the express lane, a soft-bodied man approached the officer running the counter.

The officer was having an amiable conversation with a second-level bodybuilder when the man approached.

As soon as he neared the two bodybuilders stopped their conversation, looks of disgust on their faces, the officer sent the other on his way, his baton slapping his palm, he approached the soft-bodied teen, the officer's eyes beaming down on him like the hot sun, causing him to sweat.

The officer snorted, then spat. Waving his baton lazily checked the teen's physique, muttering, "level-zero physique. Two nutritional stones for entry."

"Two?!" The soft-bodied teen frowned. "That other guy paid just one."

The officer sneered. "For working able-bodied men and women pay one price, for the lazy coasters who haven't spent a day in the gym pay double."

"I have never seen this kind of stone before, I am going to need you to hold tight while I get them appraised."

"What is the meaning of this? Those are legit stones, are you blind?" His teeth gnashed in fury.

"Next!" The officer didn't bother with the teen any longer, letting him stew off to the side broken and beaten.

The next few bodybuilders approached the officer, unbothered.

"This is just because I have no muscle!" The teen stared with bloodshot eyes. "All you bodybuilders are hateful."
