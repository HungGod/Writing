Ged held a push-up position at the summit of a mountain, his body taut, his breathing steady. 

Across his back lay a jagged slab of stone. From a distance it appeared to be ordinary rock, but up close it revealed itself as the severed tip of a mountain, hacked free and resting precariously atop him.

At the bottom of each rep, his chest brushed the ground. He lingered there for a heartbeat, muscles trembling, before driving upward again, wasting not a single motion. At seventeen, his strength and discipline surpassed even the most seasoned bodybuilders. Many called him a prodigy, his lifting feats stirring looks of admiration and envy from his peers. Yet to Ged, such praise meant very little.

He bore a rare affliction known as the Mind Shadow. Over time, it flooded his thoughts with shadows, the pressure mounting within his skull until it threatened to rupture. Left unchecked, the buildup would erupt in a single, catastrophic instant, ending his life without warning.

The only way to delay this fate was to strengthen his body faster than the Mind Shadow could swell. That relentless race left no room for rest. His life balanced on the edge of a blade, and even the smallest shift in his mood could trigger an abrupt end.

A sudden darkness swept across the mountaintop, blotting out the sun. Ged froze mid–push-up, eyes lifting skyward. An immense meteor, large as a moon, plummeted toward the horizon, its shadow swallowing the land.

“Finally. I’ve been waiting all day for this,” he murmured. Rolling his shoulders, he rose, letting the massive stone slide from his back. It thundered down the slope, unsettling rock and trailing plumes of dust in its wake.

“The Celestial Meal has arrived!” His gaze narrowed as the meteor drew closer. "I wonder who is behind this offering?"

Far beyond the atmosphere, an immense figure loomed. His physique was so vast it could be seen from the ground. His body shone with such brilliance that it cut through the darkness of the eclipsed sun, the only light in the sky.

He moved in a blur, striking poses like a bodybuilder on stage. Each stance was masterful, carrying such profound force that ripples spread across the heavens, cracks spidering across the meteor’s surface.

Ged’s gaze traced every contour, determination burning in his eyes. “Long muscle bellies in the arms… flawless delt separation… there’s no mistaking it. That form belongs to Celestial Koh!”

Koh reached the climax of his routine. The meteor quaked, fissures racing outward. Straightening, he threw both biceps wide in a grand, heavens-shattering flex. The instant his muscles locked, the meteor burst apart, scattering into a thousand molten fragments that streaked toward precise targets across the globe.

One fragment hurtled nearby, its destination the nearby town of Bowl. 

Ged’s gaze studied the fragment, his jaw tightening. “Not good… all I see are low-grade celestial rock deposits. Not a single chunk of Star Obsidian.” He exhaled a weary sigh.

He turned toward Bowl.

From afar, the city resembled a colossal, shattered stone bowl. Jagged walls enclosed crumbling buildings, their dilapidated structures better suited to shelter cockroaches than house humans. Roads splintered into winding veins, all leading to the city’s heart: a vast, fog-shrouded pit known as the Abyssal Mouth.

Across the Planet Blue Eagle, pits like this were a common sight. Its many mouths gaped in silent hunger, waiting to swallow the celestial meal hurtling toward them.

_Bang!_

The meteors slammed into the Abyssal Mouths all at once, unleashing a deafening roar. Tremors ripped through the land, splitting the earth as magma surged from the fresh cracks.

Ged clamped his hands over his ears.

Dust choked the air, staining the sky a sickly orange. Far below, a colossal rumble rolled through the earth as molten stone ground in the jaws of something beyond comprehension.

The pressure inside his skull spiked. Shadows pooled in his vision, the Mind Shadow stirring like a beast jolted from slumber. Each heartbeat felt heavier, dragging him toward the brink.

His expression hardened. His core began to glow faintly blue as he triggered _Abs Storage_. Light rippled across his midsection, the surface of his abs turning glassy and still, like a perfectly calm lake. Reaching inside, he pulled free a corked bottle containing three jagged black pills.

He clenched the bottle until his knuckles whitened, his eyes bloodshot from the smoke.  
“Damn it all… why must this planet chew so loud?”

A vein throbbed at his temple. His composure shattered, the whites of his eyes turned an abyssal shade of black. Pain lanced through his skull as glowing red fissures tore across his scalp.

Blood streaked from his eyes as he dropped to his knees, fumbling the cork free. The bottle slipped once in his trembling hands before he barely forced a pill into his mouth. He swallowed dry; the jagged edges raked his throat raw.

Shadows receded slowly. Each breath rasped in his chest, but he held himself upright as the hellish ceremony dragged on.

A grotesque swallowing sound rippled across the land, stilling the chaos for a single heartbeat.

From deep within the planet, a thunderous voice boomed:  
“Celestial Koh, your meal is much appreciated. For your contribution, you will be rewarded accordingly. The ceremony is finished; all those who have been waiting patiently may now clean up the scraps.”

In the heavens, hundreds of Cosmic Bodybuilders appeared, orbiting the planet like constellations. They flexed and inhaled in unison. The skies twisted into typhoon patterns as an incredible sucking force stripped the loose debris from the surface.

Ged ducked into a narrow crevice, wedged himself between two rocks, and braced against the storm.

Moments later, the winds died, the heat drained away by the celestial giants. With nothing else of value to extract, the Cosmic Bodybuilders vanished, and the planet began to settle.

When the last tremor faded, Ged emerged from the crevice. The cracks on his scalp sealed; the whites returned to his eyes.

After a time, he sat with the pill bottle resting in his palm. His gaze tired.  
“I can only make it halfway through a Celestial Meal before my Mind Shadow flares up… this is only a slight improvement from last time.” 

A wry smile tugged at his lips as he studied the two remaining pills.
“My mind is still not strong enough to last without pills. Without Star Obsidian to make more, I don't know how much longer I will last.”

His eyes lifted toward the Abyssal Mouth at the heart of Bowl, a flicker of resolve sparking in their depths.

He slid the vial back into his abs, brushed the dust from his clothes, and rose.  
“I hate fighting for scraps. But resources are tight and work is scarce. If what I scavenge buys me even a single extra pill…”

Blue lines flared along his calves as the muscle ability, _Mountain Walk_ activated. The soles of his feet seemed to meld with stones, keeping his posture steady as he navigated the steep mountain decline, on his way to Bowl. 

After the Celestial Meal, jagged shards had thrust through cooling magma, leaving a dangerous stretch of spikes and hissing steam between him and his destination. With _Mountain Walk_, he skimmed from shard to shard, leaping over boiling vents and crossing the broken terrain with practiced ease.

Bowl was now just a kilometer away, giant stone walls climbed skyward enclosing its interior from the outside, six fissure-like cracks served as entry points which were bursting with activity as bodybuilders and untrained civilians alike were bustling with activity. 

His steps slowed. The scent of blood filled his nostrils, and a strange sight appeared before him. 

A suited man was hurrying toward the entrance when the earth beside him ruptured. The ground split wide, and a car-sized worm burst forth in a shower of dirt and stone.

The worm’s mouth yawned open, a ring of serrated teeth glistening in the light. Inside, seated like pilots in a cockpit, were two bodybuilder thugs, each with poorly drawn tattoos of golden suns on the side of their head. One was fat, the other thin. 

“One stone, and you keep your legs,” the fat thug slurred through broken teeth, brandishing his biceps like deadly weapons.

The suited man’s expression fell, his voice a shaky whimper. "M-mr. Golden Suns gangsters. Please be reasonable, I am not a bodybuilder, how could I possibly carry the stones you require. Can I instead pay with money?" From his pockets he pulled out a wad of crumpled bills, holding it out for the thug.

The fat thug only snorted in response. "Legs it is." A vicious grin curled his lips. His arms chopped down at the man like the swinging of a scythe, the suited man let out a blood hurtling scream his upper half tumbling to the ground.

The thin thug jumped out of the worm, collecting the man's legs and storing them away in his abs. Then turning to the man, he performed a tourniquet, stemming the bleeding from the man, just enough to keep him alive. 

The two thug's let out a chuckle, before operating the worm to descend back underground, leaving the legless crippled man wailing in agony.

Ged couldn't help but furrow his brow. _So cruel!_ 

His thoughts cut short, a pulled cart appeared beside him. A black gecko-like creature pulling from reins where a young driver rode hastily. The cart hid a large group of miserable legless civilians, no longer the energy to yell out in agony, they stared hatefully out at Ged and the others who they passed by. 

Ged stared at the driver. He wore a plain white tunic, dirty, sewn on just above his heart a peak-less mountain was embroidered. Ged recognized him.

"Reverend Vick!"

He called out, waving his hands in greeting, causing the driver of the wagon to look over in surprise, relaxing his grips on the reins he commanded the lizard to halt. Ged walked over to greet him.

Vick studied Ged's approaching figure and his face softened. "You are that Novice Reverend Gore has been raising... Ged" 
He let out a soft sigh.
"It is reassuring to see a fellow member of the Church of Mount Existence. Some evil plot is unfolding. I have only traveled a short distance from the western church to pick up supplies, yet when I saw the sight of those poor souls left to bleed out and suffer on the side, I couldn't help but feel obliged to extend a helping hand." Vick shook his head at the thought.

Ged frowned. "Are those two thugs the cause of this? I don't recognize their faces, they are too ruthless. They clearly hail from outside of Bowl, yet they wear Golden Sun's tattoos. They are clearly trying to stir up trouble, but for what reason?"

Vick gave a heavy look. "These two thugs... From what I understand is that there are many such extortion operations. They all seem to focus on the untrained civilians, harvesting their legs, and leaving them behind as wailing cripples, while the bodybuilders are left mostly alone. Clearly someone is trying to sow discontent. For what there reasons are... I am sure the Golden Sun's would like to know the answer."

Ged gave a thoughtful look, but didn't respond.

Vick continued, his voice concerned, "Anyways, I should really get going. I do hope your destination is not the Abyssal Mouth. Bowl is very unstable at the moment, I fear we are but one spark away from disaster."

Vick tugged on the reins of the lizard monster, getting ready to drive his wagon. "Take care Ged, may we meet again under better circumstances."

Vick rode off to collect the recently crippled suited man, hauling him up into the back of his wagon.

Ged watched Vick leave, his eyes narrowing at the distant thugs. _I was not going to make a move, but I changed my mind. Perhaps it would be worth the effort to stop their rampage after all._ Reaching into his abs he grabbed some baggy clothing, wearing atop his existing outfit he hid away his physique, taking a few steps to the side he awaited the thugs arrival.

The thugs were emerged from the ground tens of meters away. They were in a heated conversation with a spikey-haired bodybuilder nearly twice the size of Ged. 

"And what if I refuse?" The spikey-haired bodybuilder, crossed his arms across his chest, glaring hatefully at the two thugs.

"You dare go against the golden suns?" The thin thug glared maliciously.

The large bodybuilder let out a roar, flexing his chest outwards, the head of a spear poked its way out from his body.

"Oh shit, he dares." The fat thug yelped with freight. "Retreat!"

Blue energy flowed through the two thug's hands into their fingertips, digging their hands into the worm, they desperately commanded it to leave. 

Almost instantly the mouth of the worm closed tight. The spikey-haired bodybuilder could only curse in annoyance as his mighty spear did nothing but cause a chip on the worm's iron-like teeth.

The worm burrowed into the earth, causing dust clouds to rise. The spikey-haired bodybuilder tried to stop it, his fingers alit with blue energy, trying to wrest control of the worm away from the thug, yet his efforts yielded nothing. 

The blue energy drifted off into the sky like smoke, the man let out an annoyed grunt. The worm gone. He could only return his spear back to his chest and leave.

***

Deep underground, the worm’s interior swayed gently as it tunneled. In the dim light, the two thug's sat hunched over.

"Holy shit bro." The thin thug wiped off the sweat from his brow. "I thought we were going to die." 

The fat thug let out a belly-filled laugh, "what a rush! This is what we have been missing. We have been taking too many legs from those cowardly untrained-folk. I have been itching to increase the weight on my bench press, we need to gather more stones!"

"Brother... let us not be too greedy," the thin thug reminded, "the employer said we could trade in 16 legs for a stone. Extorting these bodybuilders is too dangerous."

The fat thug's expression fell. "Too dangerous? Brother this worm is invincible! Even that lunk of a man couldn't stop us. What is there to worry about, if things get too hot, we just leave soon after."

The thin thug's expression softened.

The fat thug’s grin widened. “Now quit worrying and find us the next sucker.”

The thin thug scrambled to the back of the worm, where a teardrop-shaped uvula swayed like a fleshy periscope. Gripping it, he peered into a viewing panel, the image beyond displaying the ground above as if the earth were glass. His expression hardened. He studied each bodybuilder near the city gate, sizing them up. Then, his eyes lit up.

“Ooh… Look at this one, brother. His physique is not bad, but it is also not too good. I feel he would be an easy target.”

The fat thug raised an eyebrow. "Let me see." Moving toward the back he grabbed the uvula from the thin thug's hands, his eyes widening.

“No gang tattoos. No government markings." An excited gasp escaped his lips. "Good find! Let's get him!”

They shared a low, ugly chuckle as the fat thug gave the command for the worm to surface.

***

Ged watched the ground tremble in front of him. His stance shifted slightly.

The worm burst forth, mouth yawning wide, both thugs grinning inside with guns raised.  
For an instant, their eyes met Ged's.

The fat thug began to gesture, lips parting for his well-rehearsed extortion speech, unaware of the danger hurtling steadily toward him.

A jagged shard of stone slammed into his face. His skull burst in a spray of gore, his body dropping limp in the worm’s gullet.  

The thin thug froze, staring at the corpse, then at Ged.  

Ged hadn't moved an inch, one foot still extended from the kick that had sent the shard flying true. His gaze locked on the thug’s, cold as ice.

The thin thug went to flee.

Blue light flared from his fingertips as he pressed both hands into the worm’s palate, flooding its neural passageways with a single command: retreat.

The worm shuddered, jaws snapping shut as its body coiled to dive.

Ged blurred forward, crossing the distance in a heartbeat. His palm lit with a blue, flickering with black shadows as he pressed it to the worm’s hide, his energy drilling inwards like knives, penetrating the slick surface of the worm and tunneled inside its internal pathways. 

A battle of wills erupted.

The thin thug panicked, as he felt control of the worm slip.

Channelling more blue light into the inner pathways he supplemented his existing will inside the worm, and attempted to use his superior positioning to cut off Ged's connection.

Yet Ged’s will slid past his barricades like water through cracks, threading into hidden nerve channels the thug didn’t know existed. It struck without warning, each blow surgical and relentless, peeling away control until the thug’s presence was erased completely.

"How can this be?"
Control slipping away, the thin thug couldn't believe their invincible worm could be wrested away so simply. 
The thug’s eyes widened, disbelief giving way to dread. Only one kind of profession could possibly have the skills to do this.
"You-you're a Neuroalchemist."
