The Thought Burrowing Worm was the main way a Preceptor of the Temple gained income. Ged didn't know how many stones his father made from giving sermons, but he knew it wasn't small.

Gore's face twisted at the request.

"You want the Thought Burrowing Worm? Absolutely not. I'll trade anything else for that skull of yours, just not that."  
"If you want the Thought Burrowing Worm, become a Preceptor; then you can learn the technique from me whenever you want."

Ged felt indignation well up.

"Fine. 500 nutritional stones."

"Absolutely not!" Gore knitted his brow. "Here, how's this? I recently came across this rare workout routine. It is called Holy Sun Arms. What you have is but a simple skull of a low grade thug, don't be unreasonable."

Ged shook his head.

"And... I'll throw in 250 stones."

"50 nutritional stones, the Holy Sun Arms workout routine, and... the Thought Burrowing Worm technique... but."

"Be serious!" Gore couldn't hold his calm any longer, his hand ready to give punishment. "Otherwise I will forcefully take that damn skull from you myself, and you will get nothing."

"Wait. You didn't let me finish." Ged yelped, backpedaling a step.

"In addition to the thin thug's skull, I promise that I will become a Preceptor at a later date." Reluctance soured his face. "Just not now."

Gore's expression became thoughtful. In long, drawn-out motions, he began to stroke his beard.

"Ten years. You must head to the Golden Cathedral within the next ten years to take the Preceptor Exam. If you don't..." his eyes grew murderous. "Then, if you aren't dead already, I will kill you myself!"

“Deal,” Ged said, offering the skull.

Gore reached into his abs, he pulled out a golden tablet, as soon as it entered into the back room area, it enlightened the room in gold, he held it out for Ged.

"This is the mnemonic for the Thought Burrowing Worm. Be careful not to show it to others. What I am doing will put me at great risk to my status at the Temple. You--" he reached out to grab the skull.

_whiff._

His hand grabbed at air. Ged had retracted it back, causing Gore to howl with rage.

"Where are my stones and the workout routine? I thought we agreed to a deal," Ged said indignantly.

A vein bulged on Gore's forehead, blood visibly pumping throughout his body. He reached into his abs and took out the workout routine and 50 nutritional stones; not even waiting for Ged to respond, he yanked the skull out of his hands and handed him the items in return.

"Get out!" he roared.

Ged, frightened by his father's anger, quickly pocketed the items and hurried to the curtain doorway.

On his way out, a sharp pain shot through his behind as Gore delivered a scolding kick, causing Ged to hurry out faster.

Gore didn't stop with just one. Ged ran out yelping as several more kicks connected with his rear end.

Ged sprinted out the door. Gore chased after him onto the street. It seemed the kicks gave him some relief, his angered expression lessening.
