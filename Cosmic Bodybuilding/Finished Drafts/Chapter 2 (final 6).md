“Why did you kill my brother?” The thin thug spat. "What grudge caused you to attack us so viciously?"

Ged gave no reply, only moving closer, step by step.

The thin thug’s eyes went bloodshot. He glanced at his brother’s corpse; his throat tightened.

As Ged closed in, the thin thug’s gaze faltered, tears held back. “Damn it.”

Muscle energy surged to his fingertips. He speared both hands into the worm’s tongue. Energy raced through its neural pathways, a desperate command to flee. Ged read it at a glance.

The worm shuddered, jaws snapping shut as its body coiled to dive.

Ged was ready. He closed the distance in a heartbeat, palmed the hide, and pulsed a counter-signal inward.

The thin thug gritted his teeth. As control slipped, he flooded the worm’s pathways with muscle energy, shoring up his hold and trying to cut off Ged’s connection.

It didn’t work.

Ged’s will slid past the barricades like water through cracks, threading hidden channels and striking from blind angles. Each touch was surgical, relentless, until the thug’s presence faded entirely.

Within a few breaths, the worm answered only to Ged.

“How…?” The thug’s eyes widened, disbelief hardening into dread.

The takeover wasn’t as easy as it looked. Forcing the worm under his control overtaxed his body and mind, stirring the Mind Shadow. Ignoring the pain, he sent a command.  

The worm sagged, convulsed, then retched up the thin thug, spitting him into the dirt like bad food.

Cornered and broken, the thug flexed with a roar; his biceps ballooned, skin splitting as twin cannon barrels burst free, locking onto Ged.

“Die!” he howled, voice cracking.

A storm of bone pellets erupted, shrieking through the air at frightening speed.

Ged’s vision darkened, shadows gnawing at the edges. He flexed once, chest plates locking tight, and muscle energy armored his frame.

The bullets pinged off him like pebbles against a mountain.

The thin thug gulped.

_Step. Step._

“You’re not a civilian. Y-you're a third-level Muscle Condensation bodybuilder…!”

The thug’s arms sagged; his breath hitched as Ged loomed over him, blotting out the sun like a towering pine. Sweat dripped down his brow.

He glanced once more at his brother’s corpse, jaw trembling. Then he dropped to his knees, arms spread.

“S-sir… I was blind! I thought you were just a civilian bodybuilder—I should've never dared rob you!”

Ged froze. The desperate plea checked his step. But the Mind Shadow gnawed at his skull, and hunger for the thug’s mental treasure pulled him back to focus.

He reached into his abs, pulled the vial of Mind Soothing pills free, and swallowed the second-to-last without hesitation. He stowed the bottle and drew out a green, frog-shaped device.

The thug’s face drained. “A… a Soul Taser…” His voice cracked.

Ged flipped the frog’s mouth open, energy gathering in his palm.

“Y-you’re a neuromanifester?” the thug stammered, eyes wide. “Please, have mercy! Take my stones—take anything you want—just don’t use that device to search my mind!”

Ged sneered, pulsing muscle energy into the device.

The tongue snapped forward and struck the thug between the eyes. A faint sizzle filled the air, and he went limp, his gaze turning blank as the tether between body and soul was temporarily severed.

Ged exhaled a thin chuckle. “The Mind Shadow flare-ups are much more tolerable than before. Even on the brink of destruction, I could still manage basic tasks. That thin thug’s mental treasure really is a potent elixir.”

He studied the listless man, then let his gaze linger on the bottle containing the final Mind Soothing pill. A solemn moment passed.

Breath by breath, he waited until he was steady and the Mind Shadow had fully settled.

_I only have five minutes before the Soul Taser wears off, I need to move quick._

He scanned the area. Passersby skirted the scene; a few swung wide and shot sidelong looks. Most didn't care.

_After a scene like this, the authorities could arrive at any moment._

_With only one Mind Soothing pill left... if this search fails._  He tightened his grip on the bottle. _Doesn't matter. This mental treasure is too important. I must take this risk._

He propped the thug upright and spread his fingers across the man’s scalp. Muscle energy pulsed at his fingertips, sinking into the skin.

Closing his eyes, Ged traced each ridge and seam of the thug’s skull, reading the patterns like braille. With every contour, the impressions sank into his thoughts. As detail gathered, the thug’s neural essence swelled, and bubbles of light rose in his mind. Each bubble held a fragment of memory, glowing like a window into the past.

The fight replayed in broken flashes, each bubble capturing only a moment before fading. Soon, earlier memories surfaced: muffled voices, distorted colors. The deeper he pressed, the more the images fractured, the bubbles turning murky and incomplete.

Ged glimpsed fragments of the thug’s life in the slums, a pitiful slideshow of drifting memories. He studied each one with careful precision, searching for even the faintest trace of the treasure.

After thirty seconds and hundreds of fragments searched, Ged found nothing.

Yet he remained expressionless. This was no surprise.

The treasure had been difficult to perceive, its recognition ring no more than a faint ripple. Reality had barely acknowledged it. That meant its presence was weak and likely buried deep in recessive memories, hidden within the subconscious.

His jaw tightened.  
_Surface thoughts are useless. I’ll have to dig further._

He drove further into the thug’s scalp; strangely, no blood emerged. The skin of his scalp was like soft putty, allowing his fingers to submerge within it. Energy surged from his fingertips, flooding inward. The strain made the Mind Shadow bloom, gnawing at his skull with fresh agony.

Memory fragments burst free, spilling into Ged’s mind like fish leaping from a stream. The thug’s body convulsed, froth foaming at his lips.

At this layer the fragments were more muddled. They appeared in half-formed shapes as blurred images smeared with black ink and voices fading beneath static.

Ged’s nails scraped harder, his method growing rougher. He roared in pain as the Mind Shadow swelled toward crisis.

Nearby, passersby recoiled. Some quickened their pace; others veered wide, unwilling to cross the ground of the madman whose fingers were deep inside another's head.

But Ged noticed none of it. He was completely consumed in the mind search.

A minute and a half passed, and the flow of fragments slowed.

Pain carved red lines into his scalp. If he stopped now, he might still avoid burning his last pill.

Then, just as doubt wavered, a new fragment surfaced, and Ged's hands stopped moving.

It was opaque, unreadable by ordinary means, with faint cries buried beneath sounds of static. Ged ignored them and fixed on the corner, where a tiny hole pulsed, almost too faint to see.

His eyes glittered.  
_This must have been where the mental treasure originally burrowed in from. Which means I'm close…_

His eyes grew bloodshot. He swallowed the final Mind Soothing pill and cast the empty bottle aside without a second look.

_There!_

His hands flew forward, muscle energy flaring at his fingertips. Unconcerned by the Mind Shadow’s backlash, he burrowed deeper until he was past bone, reading directly from the brain itself.

_Boom!_

The contact unleashed a surge, shards of memory erupting into his mind in a flood. They came twisted and chaotic, like black ink bleeding through wet paper.

Deep within the recessive layers of the thug’s mind, the fragments multiplied tenfold. The pain was excruciating, yet Ged forced himself onward.

Seconds blurred into minutes. Five had already passed. His time was gone.

Ged’s throat tightened, his heartbeat thundered.  
_No! This can’t be. Where is it?_

His technique faltered, the fragments splintering before they could form. The thug’s soul clawed at the tether of his body, ready to return.

The pressure of possible failure made the Mind Shadow rebound. Red fissures split across Ged’s scalp, his head swelling toward rupture.

His breath rasped. Stopping meant dying.

“I refuse!” he roared. Summoning the last dregs of strength, he read deeper.

The thug screamed, half-conscious, as his nervous system fired wildly and his limbs thrashed.

_Crack._

His skull could no longer take the pressure of mind-reading, and gave way beneath Ged’s grip. The thug spasmed once, then went limp, eyes rolling back.

Ged's face was ashen, his head blazing and swollen to the edge of rupture. 

This was his last stand.

Trembling, he traced the hairline seam of the fracture; hidden inside was a morsel of brain matter.

Something stirred.

A strange light erupted from within, casting the world in a blue tint. Time froze. A deep, thunderous beat echoed from every direction, as if the very heart of reality were thumping.

With only a flicker of life left in him, Ged watched the heartbeat fade. In its place, another sound rose.

Ocean waves.

At first faint, lapping softly in his ears, then swelling louder and louder until it drowned out everything else.

The blue-tinted world hazed. Through the cracks, an ocean bled into view until it consumed his vision. His feet sank into fine white sand. Salty air and a warm breeze replaced the nauseating stench of stone and dust.

The ocean surged through his mind, saturating every sense until it nearly suffocated him.

Ged’s breath hitched. His eyes widened as he glanced down, seeing his body still intact.  
“Am I dead?” he whispered. Then a thought struck, freezing him in place.  
“No… this must be the mental treasure.”

Each crashing wave seemed to pass through his very soul. A crushing calm overwhelmed him while his Mind Shadow thrashed wildly.

Within his consciousness, the Mind Shadow folded into itself. With every breath, it shrank as outside pressure crumpling it inward. It roared, imploding like an eldritch beast, before falling still.

When the sensations ceased, a pin-sized black hole hung at the center of his mind.

Ged’s knees buckled. Tears spilled down his face as he gazed at the endless horizon. The ocean’s touch no longer brought pain. His mind thrummed with peace—true peace.

A broken laugh burst from his chest.  
“Father… you lied to me. You told me there was no cure, that I had to accept suffering. But look! Look at this! What suffering do I need to accept? Once this treasure is mine, that damn Mind Shadow will never harm me again!”

He turned back to the vision, desperate to seize every sensation. He tried to etch each wave, each breath of salt air, into memory.

“What’s this?”

The instant he tried to memorize it, great changes began to occur in the ocean vision.

“No.” Ged’s voice cracked.

The ocean was fracturing before his eyes. The black hole Mind Shadow began to stir, shadowy tendrils emerging from within. The vision cracked, and pieces were vanishing, disappearing like smoke.

They wouldn't stay in his mind.

Ged tried to hold on to a few of the larger fragments in his mind but found them incomparably fragile; they slipped through his grasp like sand.

Desperation mounted.

The ocean vision was about halfway gone.

Gritting his teeth, he gave up on the larger fragments; instead, he searched for the smallest.

His mind’s eye focused on a small chunk of ocean sky shaped like a diamond. Letting out a roar, he spent every last drop of his energy to seize the small ocean fragment.

It seemed simple at first, a patch of pale blue empty space reflecting the might of the vast ocean, but as he gazed into it further, he found it profoundly complex and difficult to comprehend.

The piece shook. Its absorption in his mind was slow to complete. The ocean vision was about 80% gone, and only the small piece and a few other fragments remained. Bowl slowly swam back into view.

Pain roared through his head.

The ocean vision collapsed.

Ged fell to his knees, breathless. Barely able to think, his mind was completely spent.

Yet the experience of the trip was not for nothing; in his consciousness, a blurry ocean sky fragment had taken shape.

Although he could not seize the full thing, he was just barely able to capture 20% of the ocean sky fragment.

As soon as the ocean sky fragment was captured, the Mind Shadow retracted its tendrils, its form completely hidden within the pin-sized black hole.

Ged sat in a hazy daze in front of the city gate of Bowl, unable to gauge how much time had passed.

Soon his thoughts cleared, recognizing his tight situation, he began to act.

The thin thug’s corpse slumped beside him. With a vicious twist, Ged tore off the head. Muscle energy surged through his palms, melting flesh and blood until only a bleached skull remained.

He slid the skull into his abs to study later and forced himself upright. Dizzy, but with the Mind Shadow silent, his thoughts felt sharper than ever.

Wiping his hands clean, Ged limped away from the scene, avoiding a search of the thug corpses. As soon as he had the strength, he ran toward Bowl’s fractured gates, desperate to vanish before the scene grew messy.

Muffled sirens boomed from underground.

A crimson beacon shot skyward, blazing into the shape of a giant handprint. The crowd scattered, voices breaking into frightened whispers.

Ged slowed, eyes narrowing.  
_The Equalizers._

His brow furrowed. _They're tricky to handle sometimes, but things should work out._

_But if my brother is on duty…_ His stomach knotted. _That could bring a bit of trouble._

He turned his back on the beacon and slipped into Bowl before the Equalizers surfaced.
