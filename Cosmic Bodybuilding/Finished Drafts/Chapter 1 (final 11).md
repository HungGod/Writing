At the mountain’s summit, Ged performed weighted push-ups.  

A jagged slab lay across his back. From afar, it appeared as ordinary stone, but up close it revealed itself as the severed tip of the peak, hacked free and riding his shoulder blades.

With each descent, his chest grazed the ground. He lingered for a heartbeat, muscles quivering, then drove upward again, every rep executed as if his life depended on it.  

For his age, few matched Ged’s diligence and focus in bodybuilding. Yet this devotion was not born of passion. For him, it was survival.  

He had been born with a rare affliction known as the Mind Shadow, a formless black shadow nested in his skull that fed on agitation. Day by day it swelled, pressing outward until his head could no longer hold itself together, ending his life in a gruesome rupture.

The only way to delay that fate was to strengthen his body faster than the Mind Shadow could grow. To Ged, stillness was no different from surrendering to death.

Ged froze mid-push-up, his eyes lifting skyward.

Darkness swept across the mountaintop, blotting out the sun. An immense meteor loomed above, its burning mass consuming the horizon as it tore toward the planet.

“Finally. I’ve been waiting all day for this,” he murmured. Rolling his shoulders, he rose, letting the massive stone slide from his back. It thundered down the slope, sending rocks skittering and dust pluming.

“The Celestial Offering has arrived.” He squinted at the onrushing meteor. “Which Celestial brought this month’s offering?”

Far beyond the atmosphere, an immense figure loomed. His physique was so vast it could be seen from the ground. His body shone with such brilliance it cut through the darkness of the eclipsed sun, the only light in the sky.

He moved in a blur, striking poses like a bodybuilder on stage. Each stance was masterful, carrying such profound force that ripples spread across the heavens, cracks spidering across the meteor’s surface.

Ged’s gaze traced every contour, determination burning in his eyes. “Long muscle bellies in the arms… flawless delt separation… There’s no mistaking it. That physique belongs to Celestial Koh!”

Koh reached the climax of his routine. The meteor quaked, fissures racing outward. Straightening, he threw both biceps wide in a grand, heaven-shattering flex. The instant his muscles locked, the meteor burst apart, scattering into a thousand molten fragments that streaked toward precise targets across the globe.

A fragment hurtled nearby, heading straight for the bowl-shaped city of Bowl.

Ged tracked its fall, shoulders sinking. “Star Obsidian. Celestial Iron. Moon Stone.” He clicked his tongue. “All low-grade scraps. No decent nutritional stones.”

He looked toward Bowl’s core: a fog-swallowed chasm locals called the Abyssal Mouth.

Across Planet Blue Eagle, countless Abyssal Mouths dotted the world. Each was a silent maw, waiting to swallow the fragment aimed at it.

_Bang!_

The meteors slammed into the Abyssal Mouths all at once, unleashing a deafening roar. Tremors ripped through the land, splitting the earth as magma surged from the fresh cracks.

Ged clamped his hands over his ears.

Dust choked the air, staining the sky a sickly orange. From the depths came a heavy _chomp_, then another. The planet chewed the offering, crushing the meteor fragments between myriad unseen jaws.

The chewing gnawed at Ged’s mind. Agitation surged, causing the Mind Shadow to stir. The shadow lashed out as it fed, and pressure mounted in his skull. Pressure bred agitation, which fed the shadow, which further raised the pressure, creating a vicious feedback loop.

He flexed, sending muscle energy to his skull to form a protective layer and contain the disease from further expansion.

It was not enough. Each heartbeat grew heavier; his muscle energy slowly eroded away and his condition worsened.

His expression hardened. Muscle energy gathered in his core, flashing with light as he invoked the muscle ability: Abs Storage. His abs turned glassy and still, like a calm lake. With quick practiced motions he reached inside and pulled free a corked bottle holding three jagged black pills.

His composure cracked; the whites of his eyes sank into abyssal black. Pain lanced through his skull as glowing red fissures split across his scalp.

He clenched the bottle until his knuckles whitened, his smoke-reddened eyes blazing.  
“Damn it all… why can’t this planet chew quietly?”

Blood streaked from his eyes as he dropped to his knees, fumbling the cork free. The bottle slipped once in his trembling hands before he barely forced a pill into his mouth. He swallowed dry; the jagged edges raked his throat raw.

Shadows receded slowly. Each breath rasped in his chest, but he held himself upright as the hellish ceremony dragged on.

A reverberation of swallowing echoed, as the planet finished its meal. For a heartbeat, everything went silent.

From deep within Planet Blue Eagle, a haughty voice boomed:  
“Celestial Koh, your offering is passable. Rewards will be issued through the usual channels. The ceremony has concluded. All others may pick up the scraps.”

In the heavens, hundreds of celestial bodybuilders appeared, dotting the sky like constellations. They flexed and inhaled in unison, twisting the sky into typhoon spirals as a powerful suction took hold. It siphoned the hellish heat from the air. The lava rifts cooled to fresh stone.

Moments later, the winds died. The celestial bodybuilders vanished, and the planet settled back to normal.

When the last tremor faded, Ged pushed himself to his feet. The cracks on his scalp sealed; the whites returned to his eyes.

After a while, he sat with the pill bottle resting in his palm. His gaze grew tired.  
“I can only make it halfway through a Celestial Offering before my Mind Shadow flares up… this is only a slight improvement from last time.” 

_When does this suffering end?_
A wry smile tugged at his lips as he studied the two remaining pills.
“I still can’t go without Mind Soothing pills. Every waking moment feels like waiting for death.”

His eyes lifted toward the Abyssal Mouth at the heart of Bowl, a flicker of resolve sparking in their depths.

He slid the vial back into his abs, brushed the dust from his clothes, and stood.  
“I hate fighting for scraps. But work is scarce. If scavenging buys me even one more pill…” 

He leapt from the cliff and descended toward Bowl.

The path was treacherous, littered with loose rock and sudden drop-offs, but Ged moved with practiced agility. His soles gripped the stone like a climber on a sheer wall, carrying him swiftly downslope.

In the Celestial Offering’s wake, jagged shards jutted from cooling magma, a gauntlet of spikes and hissing steam. Ged bounded from shard to shard, leaping over boiling vents and weaving through the broken terrain as if retracing a route he knew by heart.

Bowl lay minutes away. Giant stone walls enclosed a vast basin. Six fissure-gates served as entrances, each choked with traffic. Bodybuilders and untrained civilians alike bustled through.

Ged slowed to a halt, eyes narrowing. Ahead, a scene at the gate caught his attention.

A bodybuilder in a suit hurried toward the entrance when the earth beside him ruptured. The ground split wide, and a carriage-sized worm burst out in a shower of dirt and stone.

Its mouth gaped wide, ringed with serrated teeth that glistened in the light. Seated inside, like grotesque pilots, were two bodybuilder thugs: one fat, one thin.

“One nutritional stone, and you keep your legs,” the fat thug slurred through broken teeth, flexing his biceps like deadly weapons.

The suited man froze, his jaw tightening. Resignation clouded his eyes. He reached into his abs and offered a nutritional stone with trembling hands.

Ged’s gaze sharpened. As the stone changed hands, he noticed something no one else did.

A wisp of neural essence peeled from the man’s head and drifted upward like smoke. Carrying the experience of extortion, it twisted in the air before sliding into the thin thug’s skull and vanishing.

_External neural phenomenon?_ Ged frowned. _Why is it showing up here…?_

He understood such things better than most. His life with the Mind Shadow had given him an unending thirst to learn about the mind. Years of study and even an apprenticeship had given him a wide breadth of knowledge.

Neural essence is shaped by thoughts, feelings, and experience. It usually remains unseen. Only in rare cases does it escape into the outside world, forming what scholars call external neural phenomena—viewable fragments of thought.

Most neural phenomena are harmless and fade quickly. They are usually triggered by external stimuli rather than by chance. Yet this phenomenon had left the suited man and been absorbed into the thin thug’s mind. There was more to this than it seemed.

_Is that thug cultivating some kind of mental technique?_ Ged searched through his memory, his brow furrowing. 

Something flickered above the thug. A translucent seed took form, trembling before splitting open. A green sprout pushed free.

_Wait. A mental treasure…?_ Ged’s chest tightened. Such treasures were the rarest of neural phenomena. When an essence lingered instead of vanishing, it could root itself in a person’s subconscious. By feeding on stray experiences, it grew until it sprouted into a tangible treasure, acknowledged by reality itself. These treasures could take countless forms, some wondrous, some disastrous, others merely strange.

The sprout grew swiftly, blossoming into a radiant blue flower. Its bloom released a faint ring of neural essence before the flower dissolved.

_That ring represents reality's acknowledgement of that treasure's existence. When it passes through others, I should be able to get an idea of its power level. But so far, these reactions…_

Ged watched as the ring slid through each passerby, unnoticed. Even the thin thug seemed oblivious to the mental treasure blooming inside his subconscious.

He sighed. _It seems this treasure is merely a useless oddity._

He started to turn away when the blue ring from the thin thug’s treasure struck him.

A tingling wave rippled through his skull.

_Impossible!_ He reeled.

The Mind Shadow recoiled. For a heartbeat, the crushing pressure in his head lifted.

Suddenly, his mind was clear. No pain. No pressure. Only silence. For the first time, Ged felt relaxed.

Then the tingling faded. The pain returned, but lighter now, as though it had retreated a step.

His incurable affliction had weakened. The mental treasure in the thin thug’s mind carried an unfathomable tonic, more potent than any pill or technique.

Hope. For the first time in his life, he had tasted something other than despair. And now that he’d had a taste…

His lips were dry. His voice, a hungry whisper. “That treasure inside that thug’s mind…”

He fixed on the thug like a hunter stalking prey.

“I must have it.”

Up ahead, the suited bodybuilder flinched. “I’ve already paid the price. Can’t I please leave?”  

“You only paid one of us.” The thin thug sneered, palm out. “Aren’t you forgetting to pay me as well?”  

Jaw tight, the suited man produced another nutritional stone and set it in the waiting hand.

The thugs laughed at his back. The worm’s mouth closed and the ground sealed as it sank.

Ged watched the spot where they vanished, brow furrowing. _That worm is incredibly fast at retreating. Trapping him will be difficult._

After a moment’s thought, he adjusted his clothes, then stepped a few paces to the left, settled in, and waited.

***

Deep underground, the worm’s interior swayed as it tunneled. In the dim light, the thin thug hunched over, nutritional stones clinking in his palm.

“This city is amazing,” he said, biting off a chip of a nutritional stone. “Ten nutritional stones in minutes. With food like this, we’ll soon reach the peak of second-level Muscle Condensation.”

His expression clouded. “But why do you insist we push for the third-level and beyond? Are we that desperate?”

“Of course we are.” The fat thug’s face flushed. “Don’t you want the good life? What’s wrong with a little more muscle on your bones?”

The thin thug’s face fell. “Brother... you know as well as I do that training your physique beyond the second-level without a permit is a crime punishable by death. We have criminal records, we can't own permits. If the authorities catch us, what are we going to do? I hope you’ve thought this through.”

“Of course I have,” the fat thug spat. “We do what others aren’t willing to do. If the authorities find out, we bribe their bosses to overlook our cases. If that fails and they still decide to hunt us down, we bawl our eyes out until they’re too disgusted to take our heads.”

The thin thug frowned. “Brother… what are you saying?”

“Strength over dignity.” The fat thug's eyes gleamed. "Exploit the weak, lick the boots of the strong. That is the path to prosperity."

The thin thug opened his mouth, then closed it, unsure what to say.

The fat thug's grin widened. “Now quit counting the loot and find us our next victim.”

The thin thug scrambled to the back of the worm, where a teardrop-shaped periscope of flesh hung from the ceiling. He gripped it and peered into the lens. The membrane showed the ground above as if the earth were glass. His gaze narrowed. He studied each passerby near the city gate, sizing them up. Then his eyes brightened.

“Ooh… Look at this one, brother. With a physique like that, he's bound to have plenty of nutritional stones.”

“Let me see.” The fat thug shouldered in, grabbed the periscope, and peered. 

“No gang tattoos. No government markings. No artifacts. And look at that physique!" An excited gasp escaped his lips. "He’s a total goldmine!”

They shared a low, ugly chuckle as the fat thug gave the command for the worm to surface. 

***

Ged watched the ground tremble in front of him. His stance shifted slightly.

The worm burst forth, mouth yawning wide, both thugs grinning inside with arms raised threateningly.  
For an instant, their eyes met Ged's.

The fat thug drew breath for his well-rehearsed extortion speech.

_Crack._

A jagged shard slammed into his face. Skull shattered. His body folded backward into the worm’s gullet.

The thin thug froze. He glanced at his brother’s corpse, then at Ged.

Ged had barely moved. One foot remained extended from the kick that sent the shard true to its target. His gaze locked on the thin thug’s head. 

Ged’s eyes burned, his voice low and ragged:  
"Your mind has something I need."
