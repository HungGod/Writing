"Dad, are you a bodybuilder?"

Ged looked up at his father, he didn't look like those bodybuilders on the stage, his build was much thinner and his countenance weak.

The father smiled.

"Not really. I have just lived a while and remember a few pointers from a great bodybuilder I happened to pass by chance."

The sun was beginning to set. The bodybuilders on the amphitheater were wrapping up their performance. 

The father let out a stretching yawn. 

"It's time. Let's go back."

The father son pair descended the green hill. 

Ged looked up at his father, he felt something stirring in his mind.
