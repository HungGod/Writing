Ged is hardworking, always moving. He fears stillness, because of his father. His father died in state refusing food and water, unable to accept death, nor was he willing to continue to live, he secluded himself, sitting in Buddhist meditation in an unresponsive state, until one day his body just vanished. It was in this moment that Ged comprehends what is between life and death, allowing his soul to escape his body and he finds the door nestled in his mind.

Brit is Ged's girlfriend, someone who he confides his dreams and ambitions. She is content with her simple life, unlike Ged, she seeks stability and despite supporting Ged she is largely unambitious and dull, finding joy in the simple and mundane. She is an anchor to Ged who later he discards to pursue greater heights. She is deeply connected to the church of mount existence.

Resh is lazy but ambitious. He slithers around like a snake critical of the world, yet does little to work hard. He and Ged have a good relationship with. He ends up joining a gang and killing Ross, enlightening Ged the falseness of reality and the allegory of a cave.

Ross is the farm owner who is the embodiment of tough love. Him and Resh have a feud, and Ged and him don't have a great relationship either. He is devout in his religious practice of the church.

Gore is the preceptor for the church, mysterious and shifty. His practice is demon slaying and taming ambitious thoughts with strange mental magic. He is a mentor figure for both Ross and Brit with a focus on the near and ignoring the beyond.
