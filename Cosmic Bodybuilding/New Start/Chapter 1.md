*Thwack*

Inside the rolling fields of growing vegetables, a fifteen year old Ged had cut at the root of a broccoli flower. The long knife swung with practiced precision, he held the broccoli in his hands, in a daze he stopped moving just staring intently at the cut vegetable in his hand. His eyelids heavy, in a moment of weakness he gave into his exhaustion and shut his eyes.

"Ged, what the fuck are you doing?"

In the distance a red-faced, middle aged Ross yelled out, his robust body straddling a grotesque koala tractor. As if koala bear's skin had been stretched over the iron chasey of a tractor, it's mouth occasionally leaking plumes of white smoke. Trailing behind it was a platform where a burly adolescent was sorting through broccoli stems, transferring them to large basin, attached to the platform a conveyor belt sat, bridging the gap between the platform and where Ged was working. 

"We still have the market to do today, quit spacing out and get back to work."

Ged came back to his senses. Flipping the broccoli in his palm to the conveyor, he moved quickly to the next stalk.
